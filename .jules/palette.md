## 2024-05-16 - Icon Button Accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons within Shadcn UI/Tailwind combinations in this app commonly lack `aria-label` attributes out of the box, rendering them inaccessible to screen readers.
**Action:** When working on navigation or header components, proactively check for and add descriptive, localized `aria-label`s to all icon-centric interactive elements.
