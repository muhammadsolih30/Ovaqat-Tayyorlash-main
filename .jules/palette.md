## 2024-10-07 - Screen reader and keyboard navigation constraints for icon-only buttons
**Learning:** Many interactive components like dropdown toggles and modal close buttons lack semantic labels (`aria-label`) and visible keyboard focus indicators (`focus-visible`). This makes it difficult for screen reader users to understand the action of the buttons and for keyboard users to track their location on the page.
**Action:** When adding or updating icon-only buttons in the UI, always provide descriptive `aria-label` attributes (in the app's default language, e.g., Uzbek) and include visible focus states such as `focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-emerald-500`. Ensure stateful buttons like dropdown toggles also include `aria-expanded` and `aria-haspopup`.
